# Project Ranking

| Project | Community | Complexity | Activity | Approachability | Appeal |
|---------|-----------|------------|----------|-----------------|--------|
| Godot   |1          |1           |3         |2                |1       |
| Blender |2          |3           |2         |1                |2       |
| VSCode  |3          |2           |1         |2                |3       |

## General Ranking
1. Godot
2. Blender
3. VS Code

## Rationale
- **Community:**
    1. Godot
        The Godot community is very active on Discord and on their Contributor Chat. Responses are more frequent on Discord, and we both are very comfortable using Discord. The other projects do not use Discord and use other platforms we would have to gain more comfort in. Both chats are likely to give a response and are respectful.
    2. Blender
        The Blender community has active communication channels, but both are unique ways to communicate that we have not seen before. They are likely to respond on both, but on their DevTalk community, there were some contributors we noted did not receive responses, or that it took a few days. Therefore, they are below Godot for community. 
    3. VSCode
        The VSCode community is active on Stack Overflow, but can still take longer to respond than a Discord community or an active chatroom such as what Blender has. Their other community channel, VSCode Discussions, is questionable if a developer would get a response. It can take up to 24 hours, and many of the threads were not that active. We would trust either of the other communities more to provide a response.
- **Complexity**
    1. Godot
        All three of these projects feature languages or tools one or both of us are unfamiliar with. Godot seems to have very high quality documentation. Despite the skill gaps we may have, the documentation and the clear outlining of what frameworks and languages are used helps us to feel confident that we will be able to know what we need to learn and isolate those skills. 
    2. VSCode
       VSCode is similar regarding required skills and being able to figure out what we need to learn. However, the documentation is weaker than Godot's, so we ranked it below Godot.  
    3. Blender
       We were unable to find a comprehensive list of frameworks and tools required to work on Blender (excluding coding languages). Therefore, we feel like there could be unexpected skill gaps that pop up. They probably wouldn't differ much than what we would face with the other projects, however, Blender is also a 3D modeling software. We are both more comfortable working in a game engine or text editor, because we are programmers in the game design field, not artists. We worry that there could be even more skill gaps that pop up due to lack of 3D modeling knowledge, or that it would be harder for us to work on coding things related to 3D modeling.
- **Activity**
    1. VSCode
        VSCode is extemely active, scoring ~13K issues on the issue tracker, whereas Godot has ~12K, and Blender has ~7K. VSCode and Godot score closely for pull requests, but due to VSCode having the most issues labeled as good for new developers, we decided to rank VSCode first.
    2. Blender 
        Blender scores lower than Godot for pull requests and overall issues, however, there are more new first issues being created by Blender. Therefore, we rank Blender higher than Godot for that reason. 
    3. Godot
        Godot scores close to VSCode for most of its activity, but has no recent good first issues created in the issue tracker, which is a concern. 
- **Approachability**
    1. Blender
        Blender and Godot were nearly a tie. However, Blender has more new good first issues than Godot, which has none, as mentioned in the above section. Blender took longer during install spikes, it took 2 hours, while Godot took 30 minutes. However, we feel like the lack of new good first issues on Godot warrants ranking it lower despite that. Part of Blender's lengthy install spike time is also due to it taking a long time to build, which just required waiting for the code to run. 
    2. Godot
        Godot takes only 30 minutes to install and has a supportive community. Therefore, despite a lack of good first issues, we feel like we could ask in the Discord server about recommendations for issues to take on, and easily get support if we needed it while getting used to their codebase. 
    3. VSCode
        VSCode's install spike was fast, similar to Godot's, however, we are concerned about the quality of the good first issues. It seems like they are still very complex and would be difficult to get started on, so it maybe wouldn't even benefit us that much to have the good first issues. Their community is also the hardest to get in touch with, which already makes us feel like they are less approachable than Godot or Blender.
- **Appeal**
    1. Godot
        As previously stated, we both have a desire to learn C++ to build our resumes for the game development industry. We believe it would be highly beneficial to us, and this is a great opportunity to begin our exposure to C++. Godot is also a game engine, that would benefit both of our resumes to have experience using, ESPECIALLY in this way of working on the engine itself. It is distinctly different from anything on our resumes for the game industry. We also have experience working in game engines, and picking up Godot seems like it shouldn't be too steep of a learning curve for us. It is the most interesting project to us by far.
    2. Blender
        Similar to Godot, Blender provides an opportunity to learn C++. However, a 3D modeling software is less of a stand out on our resumes, and less interesting to us, because we are not 3D modelers and really have no need to become more accustomed to Blender. If we are going to learn C++, we would rather do it in the environment we are more comfortable in. 
    3. VSCode
        We just really want to learn C++, and we already have exposure to JavaScript and TypeScript. This would benefit our resumes the least, and a text editor for code is also the least interesting software to us out of all of these.
