# Project Selection

## Project Selected:
Godot

## Rationale:
Despite concerns regarding the lack of new good first issues popping up, Godot seems the most in our comfort zone, considering we want to learn C++. Additionally, it is the most beneficial to our resumes as game developers. The community and documentation are strong as well, so that will help us to get started. We are weighing appeal and community the highest, due to our desire to learn a new language. Community is always important because we want to be able to get help when we need it. Appeal is also very important to us, because being interested in the project and motivated by improving our resumes for game development will help us to feel passionate about working on the project.

## Install Estimate: 
30 minutes

## Knowledge Gaps:
We both need to learn C++ and try out using Godot as a game engine, but our goal in selecting this project is to gain these skills. We also will need to familiarize ourselves with the libraries and frameworks that they use, since neither of us are familiar with any of those. 

## Concerns:
We are concerned regarding the lack of new good first issues popping up on their issue tracker, however, we are willing to sift through the issues and find things that seem approachable, or to approach the community and ask for suggestions or what issues people worked on first when they joined the project. We also are concerned about learning C++, but it is something we want to do, so we are just going to jump in and try it.
